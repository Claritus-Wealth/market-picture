Markets

Total return in sterling, in percent: price change plus dividends reinvested on the ex-dividend date, so distributing and accumulating lines compare fairly. Each figure runs from the last close on or before the period start to the as-at date. From high is the distance below the highest point of the last 52 weeks, on the same total-return basis (0.0 = at the high). Click a heading to rank every line by it; click again to reverse, a third time to return to layers.

Month-ends and quarter-ends

The same total return, but for whole calendar months and quarters: each figure runs from the last close on or before one month-end (or quarter-end) to the last close on or before the next, so it lines up with platform valuation dates and quarter-end reports. Rates show the change in basis points. A month appears once its last weekday has closed.

Rates

Yield in percent; changes in basis points (100 bp = 1 percentage point). Blue means the yield rose, which is a fall in bond prices.

Relationships

How one part of the market did against another. Market pairs show relative total return (positive: the first did better). Rate pairs show the spread and how it moved, in basis points.
